Apache NetBeans libraries are covered by LICENSE, NOTICE and licenses/.

OpenJFX (JavaFX)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version:   @JAVAFX@, for macOS aarch64, macOS x64, Windows x64 and Linux x64
Licence:   GNU General Public License, version 2, with the Classpath Exception
Upstream:  https://openjfx.io/ - source: https://github.com/openjdk/jfx
SDKs:      @URL@/openjfx-@JAVAFX@_osx-aarch64_bin-sdk.zip
             SHA-256 @SHA256_MACOS_AARCH64@
           @URL@/openjfx-@JAVAFX@_osx-x64_bin-sdk.zip
             SHA-256 @SHA256_MACOS_X64@
           @URL@/openjfx-@JAVAFX@_windows-x64_bin-sdk.zip
             SHA-256 @SHA256_WINDOWS_X64@
           @URL@/openjfx-@JAVAFX@_linux-x64_bin-sdk.zip
             SHA-256 @SHA256_LINUX_X64@

Bundled files (the licence texts are in nbfx-libs/legal/javafx/):
@FILES@

The files are those of the SDKs above, unmodified, with one exception:
javafx.graphics.jar differs per platform, so the one here is the union of the
four SDKs' javafx.graphics.jar. The entries the platforms share are identical
in all four; the platform-specific ones (e.g. com/sun/glass/ui/mac, win, gtk)
are each taken from their SDK. The other jars are those of the Linux x64 SDK.
The native libraries are in modules/lib (Windows, Linux), modules/lib/aarch64
and modules/lib/x86_64 (macOS).

javafx.web (WebKit), javafx.media, javafx.swing and javafx-swt are NOT bundled.

The corresponding source code of the OpenJFX modules is available from the
upstream repository above, at the tag of version @JAVAFX@.
